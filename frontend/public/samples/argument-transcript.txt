JUDGE NGUYEN: Counsel, help me understand the timeline. Your officers say the
suspect reached toward his waistband, but the dash-cam footage — Exhibit 12 —
shows both hands on the steering wheel at the moment the first shot is fired.
How do you square that?

MR. HALE: Your Honor, the footage is taken from an angle that does not capture
the full range of motion —

JUDGE NGUYEN: I've watched it several times. I'm skeptical. The court is being
asked to accept an account that the record itself appears to contradict.

MR. HALE: We would submit that under Graham the perspective that matters is
that of a reasonable officer on the scene, and that the officers perceived a
threat in a rapidly evolving situation.

JUDGE BADE: But Graham doesn't require us to accept a perception the video
undercuts. Doesn't the "clearly established" inquiry under Harlow presume we
take the facts in the light most favorable to the plaintiff at this stage?

MR. HALE: It does, Your Honor.
